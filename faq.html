No. ANDY98563 is an early-stage financial technology concept brand, not a licensed bank or financial institution.

No. The transfer experience is a simulation only. No real money moves.

No. All balances, cards and charts use illustrative sample data.

No. The virtual card is a visual concept and is not issued by any card network or financial institution.

No. The contact form is a frontend demonstration and does not send or save messages.

No launch date has been announced. Any real financial services would require appropriate legal, technical, regulatory and partner arrangements.
